# Introduction to Multimodal AI
This course item introduces the concept of multimodal AI, explaining its evolution, core components, applications, challenges, and future trends.

## Definition and Evolution of Multimodal AI
- Multimodal AI systems process and understand multiple types of data simultaneously, such as text, images, audio, and video, mimicking human sensory integration.
- Early AI models specialized in single modalities, but modern models like OpenAI's CLIP and GPT-4.1 integrate multiple modalities for unified understanding.

## Core Components and Functionality
- Key functionalities include text processing, computer vision, speech processing, text-to-speech, and multimodal fusion to combine data types.
- The workflow involves separate encoding of each modality, feature extraction, alignment of data, fusion for context understanding, and unified output generation.

### Applications, Challenges, and Future Trends
- Applications span virtual assistants, healthcare diagnostics, education, autonomous vehicles, content creation, and accessibility improvements.
- Challenges include data alignment, modality imbalance, high computational demands, interpretability, bias, and privacy concerns.
- Future trends focus on unified models, edge computing, self-supervised learning, personalization, and ethical AI development.
